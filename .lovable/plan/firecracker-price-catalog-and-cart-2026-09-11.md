# Firecracker price catalog and cart

## Build
- Replace the blank home page with a festive firecracker shop.
- Show six products with clear photos, prices, pack details, and add-to-cart controls.
- Add a cart drawer that supports quantity changes, removal, item count, subtotal, and total.
- Keep the layout compact and easy to use on phones, while adapting cleanly to desktop.
- Add page-specific title and social metadata.

## Visual direction
- Use a lively Indian festive palette with warm red, saffron, yellow, and deep ink tones.
- Use crisp product photography, strong price hierarchy, and restrained decorative spark motifs.
- Include small motion for cart and product interactions, with reduced-motion support.

## Technical details
- Store the cart in page state; no account, checkout, or payment connection is included.
- Define all colors, typography, and shadows as semantic design tokens in the global style system.
- Validate the finished page at mobile and desktop sizes.
